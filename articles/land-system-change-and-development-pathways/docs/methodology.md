# Methodology

This folder uses transparent scoring heuristics to represent land-system change as a development pathway risk problem.

The workflows combine:

- conversion pressure
- land degradation
- fragmentation risk
- biodiversity-function loss
- food and settlement dependence
- infrastructure expansion pressure
- justice exposure
- governance capacity
- restoration readiness

The scoring model is not intended to replace official land-system assessment. It is an explanatory and reproducible framework for article-level analysis, teaching, and further extension.
