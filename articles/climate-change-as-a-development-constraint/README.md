# Climate Change as a Development Constraint

Advanced code workflows for the article on climate change as a development constraint.

## Included workflows

- Python:
  - climate constraint and development risk scoring
  - governance, resilience, and exposure diagnostics
- R:
  - cross-region and cross-territory climate summaries
  - inequality exposure and governance gap analysis
- SQL:
  - climate-risk registry
  - hazard, burden, and governance logs
  - resilience dashboards
- Go:
  - lightweight climate-risk scoring service
- Rust:
  - validation utility for climate and development-risk records
- Embedded C:
  - optional low-resource climate signal node
- C++:
  - optional local climate-risk scorer
