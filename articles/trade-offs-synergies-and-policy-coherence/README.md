# Trade-Offs, Synergies, and Policy Coherence

Advanced code workflows for the article on trade-offs, synergies, and policy coherence.

## Included workflows

- Python:
  - policy-coherence and interaction-risk scoring
  - spillover and coordination diagnostics
- R:
  - cross-region and cross-territory policy-coherence summaries
  - spillover, sequencing, and governance-risk analysis
- SQL:
  - policy-coherence registry
  - spillover, coordination, and governance logs
  - coherence dashboards
- Go:
  - lightweight policy-coherence risk scoring service
- Rust:
  - validation utility for policy-coherence records
- Embedded C:
  - optional low-resource spillover signal node
- C++:
  - optional local policy-coherence risk scorer
