# From Economic Growth to Human Development

Advanced code workflows for the article on the shift from economic growth to human development.

## Included workflows

- Python:
  - human-development and growth-translation risk scoring
  - capability-conversion diagnostics
- R:
  - cross-region and cross-territory human-development summaries
  - HDI translation, capability expansion, and governance-risk analysis
- SQL:
  - human-development registry
  - capability, conversion, and governance logs
  - human-development dashboards
- Go:
  - lightweight human-development risk scoring service
- Rust:
  - validation utility for human-development records
- Embedded C:
  - optional low-resource capability signal node
- C++:
  - optional local human-development risk scorer
