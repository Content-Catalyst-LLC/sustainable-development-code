# Freshwater Change and Development Risk

Advanced code workflows for the article on freshwater change and development risk.

## Included workflows

- Python:
  - freshwater change and development risk scoring
  - monitoring, governance, and water-system burden diagnostics
- R:
  - cross-region and cross-territory freshwater summaries
  - hydrological exposure and governance gap analysis
- SQL:
  - freshwater-risk registry
  - streamflow, ecosystem, and governance logs
  - water-burden dashboards
- Go:
  - lightweight freshwater-risk scoring service
- Rust:
  - validation utility for freshwater and development-risk records
- Embedded C:
  - optional low-resource hydrological signal node
- C++:
  - optional local freshwater-risk scorer
