# Methodology

This folder uses transparent scoring heuristics to represent freshwater change as a development-risk problem.

The workflows combine:

- streamflow stress
- soil-moisture stress
- water-quality burden
- wastewater-treatment deficit
- freshwater-ecosystem decline
- food and livelihood dependence
- health and sanitation exposure
- governance capacity
- monitoring readiness

The scoring model is not intended to replace official hydrological assessment, SDG monitoring, or water-governance analysis. It is an explanatory and reproducible framework for article-level analysis, teaching, and further extension.
