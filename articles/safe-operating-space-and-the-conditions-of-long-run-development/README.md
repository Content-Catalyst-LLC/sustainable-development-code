# Safe Operating Space and the Conditions of Long-Run Development

Advanced code workflows for the article on safe operating space and the conditions of long-run development.

## Included workflows

- Python:
  - safe-operating-space and long-run development risk scoring
  - boundary-pressure and resilience diagnostics
- R:
  - cross-region and cross-territory safe-operating-space summaries
  - boundary-pressure, resilience-loss, and governance-risk analysis
- SQL:
  - safe-operating-space registry
  - boundary, resilience, and governance logs
  - long-run development dashboards
- Go:
  - lightweight long-run development risk scoring service
- Rust:
  - validation utility for safe-operating-space records
- Embedded C:
  - optional low-resource boundary signal node
- C++:
  - optional local long-run development risk scorer
