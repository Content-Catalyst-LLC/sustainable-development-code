# Industrial Policy and Sustainable Structural Transformation

Advanced code workflows for the article on industrial policy and sustainable structural transformation.

## Included workflows

- Python:
  - structural transformation scoring
  - sector upgrading and green-transition readiness
  - lock-in and diversification risk diagnostics
- R:
  - cross-country and regional industrial transition summaries
  - territorial inequality and employment-shift analysis
- SQL:
  - industrial ecosystem registry
  - policy-support instruments
  - standards and compliance tracking
  - regional and sector dashboards
- Go:
  - lightweight sector-readiness scoring service
- Rust:
  - validation utility for industrial ecosystem and policy records
- Embedded C:
  - optional low-resource industrial reporting stub
- C++:
  - optional local industrial quality and interoperability scoring

## Folder layout

- `python/`: scoring, diagnostics, and scenario workflows
- `r/`: comparative summaries and territorial analysis
- `sql/`: schema, registries, logs, and dashboards
- `go/`: lightweight scoring service
- `rust/`: typed validation tooling
- `c/`: optional embedded reporting logic
- `cpp/`: optional local industrial scoring
- `data/raw/`: source datasets
- `data/processed/`: transformed datasets
- `outputs/`: exported summaries
- `docs/`: notes and implementation guidance
