# Methodological notes

The viability score is an illustrative composite indicator, not a universal measure of development.

Use it to structure questions:

- Does output growth come with ecological decline?
- Are resilience and governance capacity improving?
- Does technological capability reduce or intensify dependence?
- Are justice and equity treated as central to viability?
- Does pressure cross ecological or institutional thresholds?
- Are policy levers robust across multiple futures?

Composite indicators should always be paired with component-level inspection, uncertainty analysis, sensitivity analysis, and participatory review.
