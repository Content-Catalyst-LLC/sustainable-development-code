# Scenario review checklist

- [ ] Scenario names describe distinct futures rather than minor variants.
- [ ] Indicators are normalized and documented.
- [ ] Weights are explicit and reviewable.
- [ ] Threshold assumptions are visible.
- [ ] High-risk pathways are not hidden by composite averages.
- [ ] Inequality and ecological integrity are evaluated directly.
- [ ] Governance capacity is treated as a system condition.
- [ ] Technology capability is distinguished from technology access.
- [ ] Results are interpreted as stress tests, not predictions.
